Unsloth 2025.3.19 introduces Fast Mistral patching with Transformers 4.51.0, delivering a 2× faster free fine-tuning experience. Running on a Tesla P100-PCIE-16GB GPU under Linux, it leverages Torch 2.5.1+cu121, CUDA 6.0 with CUDA Toolkit 12.1, and Triton 3.1.0. Unsloth 2025.3.19 patched 32 layers with 32 QKV layers, 32 O layers and 32 MLP layers. The training setup used one GPU with a maximum memory of 15.888 GB, processed 2,274 examples over 1 epoch with 284 total steps, a per-device batch size of 2, and gradient accumulation over 4 steps, resulting in an effective total batch size of 8. Despite training only 0.60% of the total 7 billion parameters (41,943,040 trainable), the system smartly offloads gradients to conserve VRAM, completing the training in 5 hours, 47 minutes, and 25 seconds at a speed of 0.01 iterations per second.
