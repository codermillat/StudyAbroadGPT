## 7. Decision rules for the §3 stratified test (pre-registration §6.4)

- **If H1 holds in Stratum W (p < 0.05, LoRA > base):** the data attribution is **established**.
- **If direction-consistent in W but under-powered:** report as "indicative."
- **If H1 fails in W (LoRA not worse than base even on wrong-context prompts):** v4's data attribution weakens. The §4.4.1 causal match remains valid; the question is whether the *aggregate* error rate is higher in W than in C.
- **If Stratum C also shows LoRA > base:** the model is uniformly worse, not data-pipeline-driven.

These rules are *already* in `paper/v5-draft.tex` §4.5 and `docs/analysis-plans/2026-09-02-stratified-causal-test.md` §6.4. They are pre-registered; deviations must be logged as amendments.
